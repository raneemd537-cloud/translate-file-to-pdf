
Small Business Plan

Project Name: Fresh Juice Shop

Project Description:
Fresh Juice Shop is a small business that sells fresh fruit juices.

Target Customers:
Students, families, and people who prefer healthy drinks.

Products:
- Orange Juice
- Mango Juice
- Strawberry Juice

Marketing Plan:
The shop will use social media to share product photos and special offers.

Project Goal:
To provide fresh drinks at reasonable prices and build a successful small business.
